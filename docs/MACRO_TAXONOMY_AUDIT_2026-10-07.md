# Macroeconomic Indicator Taxonomy Audit

**Read-only phase 1 research and diagnosis** · Inspected 7 October 2026 · Repository, UI hierarchy, source routing and local SQLite snapshot.

> No project code, catalog, configuration, or database was changed for this audit. The local DB was inspected read-only. This is not a point-by-point recalculation of all observations against upstream releases, and it does not inspect a remote production DB.

## Executive verdict

**The dashboard has a strong macro dataset, but the taxonomy is not yet fully defensible as a central-bank-oriented product. Verdict: KEEP WITH MAJOR MODIFICATIONS.** Coverage is strongest for inflation baskets, GDP expenditure detail and labour outcomes. The top-level menu is limited to Inflation/Growth/Jobs; monetary and financial transmission is missing; expectations are mixed with inflation and realized wages; the detail tier is too broad; and the catalog contains exact duplicates, broken comparison IDs, mislabels and empty feeds.

Catalog: **349 metrics** (US 174, UK 98, EA 77). Country dashboard card/component/chart definitions reference 241 unique regional IDs (US 113, UK 74, EA 54); including all country-specific keys/headline/comparison references, there are 259 (US 117, UK 84, EA 59). Across all inspected configuration, 272 catalog IDs are referenced; 13 appear only in comparison configuration and 77 have no explicit dashboard/tree/comparison reference. Database: **349 metric rows, 138,900 observations, 532 releases, 0 raw calendar events**. 23 catalog metrics have zero observations; 19 are on country pages. All catalog metric metadata rows exist in SQLite, but metadata sync does not validate official definitions or transforms.

### Ten highest-priority changes

1. Add Monetary & Financial Conditions: policy rates, overnight rates, sovereign curves/real yields, bank lending rates and credit, lending standards, FX, spreads and financial conditions.
2. Separate observed price indexes, underlying inflation, producer/import costs, expectations and wage-cost transmission.
3. Anchor each region to its own target concept: US PCE, UK CPI, EA HICP. CPI/CPIH/HICP are not interchangeable.
4. Move realized AHE/ECI/Atlanta wage growth, UK AWE and ECB Wage Tracker to Labour; keep survey wage expectations in Expectations.
5. Canonicalize eight identical aliases: NY Fed SCE 1/3/5Y, ECB SPF 1Y/LT/core 1Y, Cleveland duplicated 5Y, UK J5EC duplicate/mislabel.
6. Fix desk matrix IDs absent from catalog, generic CPI naming, survey-population mismatches, and the realized-vs-expected wage comparison.
7. Split expectations by consumer, firm, professional, market and model; market inflation compensation is not pure expected inflation.
8. Retain official sub-baskets as drilldowns; do not promote every CPI/PCE/retail detail to equal-tier cards.
9. Repair or retire zero-data feeds before showing as implemented: short US breakevens, UK 5Y/10Y compensation, EA LSEG compensation and PMIs.
10. Label geography accurately: German unemployment is not an EA aggregate.

## 2. Official frameworks and country differences

### United States — Fed / official agencies

The FOMC’s longer-run strategy specifies 2% inflation measured by the **PCE price index**; the mandate covers price stability and maximum employment. The Fed’s Monetary Policy Report covers prices, labour supply/demand, wages/productivity, GDP and its components, housing, financial conditions, credit/lending and money. PCE should anchor the US inflation group; CPI is important corroborating data, not the Fed target. BEA supplies PCE/NIPA; BLS CPI, ECI, CES/CPS, JOLTS and productivity; Census housing/retail; Fed G.17 and official district surveys. ISM and S&P PMIs are private surveys, not official statistical releases. Distinguish establishment payrolls from household employment, ECI from AHE, BEA personal consumption from Census retail sales, and GDPNow from released GDP.

### United Kingdom — BoE / ONS

The BoE targets **CPI inflation**. CPIH adds owner-occupiers’ housing costs and is a useful separate household measure, not the MPC target. RPI is a legacy index, not a comparable target series. BoE policy analysis emphasizes services inflation, pay, expectations, labour slack, productivity, activity and financial conditions. ONS monthly GDP is an important UK-specific leading/high-frequency measure alongside quarterly GDP. Keep ONS Labour Force Survey employment separate from administrative PAYE payroll employment; AWE regular/total pay and DMP realized/expected wages also differ. BoE/Ipsos IAS, DMP and MaPS have separate respondent populations.

### Euro Area — ECB / Eurostat

The ECB strategy explicitly combines **economic analysis** with **monetary and financial analysis**; omitting rates, money, lending, credit and transmission is a material gap. HICP is the price-stability measure; HICPX and HICP components matter for underlying price dynamics. ECB projections cover inflation, GDP, wages/compensation, unit labour costs, profits, import prices, productivity, unemployment and risk. CES=households, SAFE=firms, SPF=professional forecasts, Wage Tracker=realized negotiated wages, Bank Lending Survey=bank lending conditions. SPF releases quarterly even when target periods are annual. Eurostat is the official statistical backbone. Germany unemployment is national context, not EA aggregate.

### Cross-central-bank comparison

| Topic | Fed | BoE | ECB | Taxonomy consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Target inflation | PCE 2% objective; CPI monitored | CPI 2% target | HICP 2% target | Common concept, region-native measures and decomposition. |
| Underlying inflation | Core PCE; trimmed/sticky CPI supporting | Services inflation/pay persistence; CPIH distinct | HICPX plus services/NEIG/food/energy | Do not equate a generic “core CPI” across all regions. |
| Labour | CES/CPS, JOLTS, claims, wages, hours/productivity | LFS, PAYE, vacancies, AWE, slack/productivity | Eurostat LFS/vacancies, compensation/LCI/negotiated pay | Keep population, source system, and frequency visible. |
| Activity | BEA quarterly accounts + Census/BLS monthly signals | Quarterly plus ONS monthly GDP | Eurostat quarterly accounts and monthly activity/sentiment | Keep native cadence and methods; do not interpolate. |
| Monetary/financial | Fed funds, Treasury/mortgage rates, credit/lending, financial conditions | Bank Rate, SONIA/gilts, sterling, mortgages/credit | Two-pillar analysis, M3, credit, BLS, rates | Add missing top-level domain. |
| Expectations | NY Fed, Michigan, Atlanta, Cleveland, SPF, markets | IAS, DMP, MaPS, market curve | CES, SAFE, SPF, markets | Keep respondent type/horizon separate. |
| External/housing | Shelter/OER, starts, FX/trade/import costs | CPIH OOH, house/mortgage conditions, GBP/trade | Rents/house prices, housing investment, EUR/imports | Same drivers, different official measures. |
| Supply/slack | Employment, capacity, potential-output estimates | Labour slack, productivity, capacity | Output gap, productivity, unit labour costs | Model estimates require method, vintage, uncertainty labels. |

## 3. Data availability and taxonomy mismatch

Frequency distribution in the catalog: US monthly 125, quarterly 42, daily 5, weekly 2; UK monthly 80, quarterly 14, daily 3, annual 1; EA monthly 37, quarterly 33, daily 5, annual 2. A broad stored-interval scan found no series with repeated gaps grossly inconsistent with the declared cadence. This checks stored spacing, not the correctness of all source definitions. US daily breakevens (5Y/10Y/5Y5Y) run through 6 Oct 2026; weekly initial/continuing claims through 26/19 Sep; UK 5Y5Y daily through 6 Oct. US 1Y/2Y breakevens, UK 5Y/10Y compensation, all EA LSEG compensation are daily-tagged but have no rows.

Quarterly rows at `2026-04-01` represent Q2 period start, not Q1 stale data. Eurostat’s Q2 release supports that interpretation. SPF dates such as 2027/2028 are forecast target periods, not realized macro readings: store/display survey vintage separately from target date.

### Other source/name/region issues

- **US CPI:** `us-cpi` is a SA CPI-U index; separate NSA CPI series feeds conventional YoY. Expose index, SA m/m and NSA 12-month rates distinctly; do not call index level the inflation rate.
- **UK J5EC:** ONS J5EC is all-retail volume m/m including fuel. “Total ex Fuel” is a false label; use J5EC once, or wire a separate official ex-fuel measure.
- **Cleveland EXPINF5YR:** A 5-year measure, not 5–10-year forward. Rename to 5Y; collapse alias.
- **ECB Wage Tracker:** Monthly negotiated wage growth is realized wage outcome. Move from Inflation › Wage Expectations to Labour › Wages.
- **Germany unemployment:** National Germany series held with region=EA. Separate national context and prefer Eurostat harmonized series.
- **US CPI tree:** Medical-care commodities is shown within core services. Correct hierarchy; commodities/goods are not services.
- **SPF cadence:** Quarterly survey, annual/current-year target observations. Store forecast target and survey vintage independently.
- **UK GDP:** Monthly GDP and quarterly GDP are separate useful frequencies. Group in UK activity with their distinct cadence, not duplicate peer cards.

## 4. Missing indicator assessment

The current dataset lacks a coherent monetary/financial branch and several cross-country policy variables. Available official series do not automatically mean free API access or already ingested data.

| Priority | Missing indicator/group | Regions | Why / official basis | Availability / status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Policy rate: Fed target/effective rate; BoE Bank Rate; ECB deposit facility | All | Policy instrument central to FOMC/MPC/Governing Council decisions. | Missing catalog; official daily/decision-date histories public and feasible. |
| P0 | Money-market rates, sovereign yield curves, real yields, mortgage/credit rates | All | Transmission and market expectations, examined in central-bank reports. | No coherent rate curve; official Fed/Treasury/BoE/ECB sources feasible. |
| P0 | Bank credit growth/volumes, lending rates and standards | All | Household/business financing conditions; ECB monetary analysis and BLS, Fed SLOOS/credit, BoE credit monitoring. | Missing systematic branch; H.8/G.19/SLOOS, BoE and ECB MIR/BLS public. |
| P0 | Effective exchange rates | All | Imported inflation, competitiveness, trade and financial conditions. | Missing; official BoE/ECB/FRED histories feasible. |
| P0 | Productivity, hours/output and unit labour cost decomposition | All | Supply capacity and wage-to-price pass-through in all three policy frameworks. | Only partial US ULC; BLS/ONS/Eurostat series feasible. |
| P0 | Real household disposable income, savings, household credit/debt service | All | Consumption durability and demand/inflation outlook (BEA, BoE MPR, ECB projections). | SCE expectations are not realized accounts; add official BEA/ONS/Eurostat/credit. |
| P0 | Official central-bank projection vintages / uncertainty | Fed/BoE/ECB | Outlook and reaction function are central to policy. | Only partial SPF; SEP, BoE MPR and ECB projection tables accessible but vintage-aware. |
| P1 | Output gap/spare capacity/potential output | All | Slack and maximum employment; model-based, not directly observed. | Partial US capacity utilization; add estimates with vintage/uncertainty. |
| P1 | Import prices, imported inflation, energy/commodity costs | All | Separates domestic from external pressure; explicit in ECB projections and Fed/BoE reports. | US/UK partial; cross-country series incomplete, official sources available. |
| P1 | House prices/rents, mortgage rates, housing affordability/investment | All | Shelter, household balance sheets and monetary transmission. | US starts/permits only partial; systematic UK/EA/US pricing and rates missing. |
| P1 | Current account and harmonized trade/external demand | All | GDP net trade is not equivalent to external balance/current account. | Some GDP trade components exist, coherent branch absent. |
| P1 | Financial stress: spreads, bank funding, asset conditions | All | Policy transmission/risk in Fed/ECB/BoE outlooks. | No defined sourced branch; public rates/index data feasible. |
| P1 | Fiscal stance, revenue, balance, debt, transfers | All | Fiscal assumptions affect demand and projections. | Government consumption present; stance/debt/tax composition missing. |
| P2 | Business investment intentions, credit constraints, orders/capacity | All, source-specific | Useful secondary leading context. | Some regional Fed surveys/PMIs; add selectively with source type. |
| P2 | Per-capita income/GDP and demographic labour supply | All | Medium-run standard-of-living/potential context, not release-day trigger. | Official population/accounts feasible; formula must be explicit. |
| P2 | Detailed regional/sector prices | Country-specific | Explains composition but can overwhelm comparison. | Some already present; keep as drilldown. |

Availability classes: (1) concept and observations already implemented: most core HICP/CPI/PCE, GDP, labour series; (2) DB but not surfaced: many support PPI/wage/survey/expenditure rows; (3) official source exists but no configured ingestion: policy rates, credit, FX, productivity, housing and empty feeds; (4) model-defined concept: output gap/financial conditions/policy stance require explicit published methodology and vintage, not fabricated raw series.

**Official-source feasibility cross-check for the missing policy/financial layer:** the Fed publishes the effective federal funds rate and Treasury/TIPS yields via its [H.15 release](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/H15/Current/) and documents the target range on its [policy-rate page](https://www.federalreserve.gov/economy-at-a-glance-policy-rate.htm); the BoE publishes [Bank Rate and yield-curve statistics](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/statistics/yield-curves) (including nominal/real curves) and official lending/credit data; the ECB publishes the [deposit facility rate as a daily series](https://data.ecb.europa.eu/data/datasets/FM/FM.D.U2.EUR.4F.KR.DFR.LEV), [bank interest rates](https://data.ecb.europa.eu/key-figures/money-credit-and-banking/bank-interest-rates/loans), and [money/credit aggregates](https://data.ecb.europa.eu/key-figures/money-credit-and-banking), alongside its [Bank Lending Survey](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_surveys/bank_lending_survey/html/index.en.html). These confirm official series exist and are ingestible; they do not imply all feeds are already integrated, that all endpoints are equally convenient/free at scale, or that model-based “financial conditions” has a single official series.

### D. Common cross-country taxonomy
```text
Prices & Inflation → native target measure (US PCE / UK CPI / EA HICP)
  → native core/underlying → aligned price components → producer/import costs
Economic Activity & Demand → real GDP → household demand/business investment
  → government demand → production/retail → external demand/trade
Labour Market, Wages & Productivity → employment/unemployment/participation
  → vacancies/slack/hours → wages/labour costs → productivity/ULC
Monetary & Financial Conditions → policy/short rates → curves/real yields
  → bank rates/credit/lending standards → FX/spreads/conditions
Expectations & Outlook → consumers/firms/professionals/markets/models
  → official projections, vintages and uncertainty
External, Housing & Fiscal Context → trade/current account/import prices/FX
  → housing prices/rents/credit → fiscal stance/debt
```

The common layer compares concepts, not identical series. Every headline cell must retain its native measure, transformation and cadence; do not compare consumer responses to SPF or market-implied compensation as one series.

## 6. Metric-by-metric classification appendix

Classification: CORE=direct target/mandate/primary policy evidence; IMPORTANT=official and materially useful secondary measure; SUPPORTING=secondary/private survey; OPTIONAL=lower-priority context; REDUNDANT=exact alias; MISCLASSIFIED=wrong label/category/geography/interpretation; TOO GRANULAR=valid detailed series better kept below headline tier. This role classification is independent of feed health. Catalog `subcategory` is shown as current component; UI placement is separately shown. `DB rows (latest)` is local SQLite coverage. ## 9. Executive answers

- **Is the current taxonomy broadly correct?** Core indicators are relevant, but the product framework is incomplete. **KEEP WITH MAJOR MODIFICATIONS.**
- **Structurally correct proportion?** Roughly **65–75%** of indicators fit sensible macro domains, counting many granular series as valid drilldowns. This is an expert estimate, not measured accuracy.
- **10 most important changes:** see Executive Verdict: add financial conditions; split price/expectations/wages; use native targets; move realized wages; deduplicate; fix matrix; split respondent types; demote micro-baskets; repair empty feeds; correct geography.
- **10 important missing groups:** policy rates; yield curves/real rates; bank lending/credit/standards; FX; productivity/ULC; disposable income/savings; official projection vintages; output gap/slack; housing prices/rents/mortgage terms; current account/import prices/global costs.
